About

Why Cargo Hub exists.

India?s agricultural infrastructure is not short on potential. It is short on coordination. Cargo Hub exists to create the intelligent coordination layer between farmers, mandis, storage, transport, public systems and capital.

The problem

Fragmented systems, scattered capacity and disjointed operations create friction at every step of the post-harvest cycle. The real challenge is not building more isolated tools ? it is creating a connected operational layer that makes sense of the entire system.

The approach

Cargo Hub connects the physical network of agriculture with digital coordination: booking, discovery, intelligence and public infrastructure alignment without forcing a single stakeholder to manage everything alone.

The vision

To build a resilient, intelligent and scalable post-harvest infrastructure network that makes agricultural supply chains more efficient, transparent and accessible for the next decade of India?s agriculture story.
